┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ HOW PROBABILITY IS CALCULATED:                                  │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  Probability = (Pattern Quality × 40%)
              + (Momentum Score × 20%)
              + (Volatility Score × 20%)
              + (Risk/Reward Score × 20%)
              + Pattern Bonus (+5-8%)

  Only trades with Probability ≥ 75% are taken!

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ POSITION SIZING (Fractional Kelly):                             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  Risk Amount = min(Equity × 4%, Equity × 0.25%)

  Kelly Fraction = Probability - (1-Probability)/Risk:Reward

  Position Size = (Risk Amount / Stop Distance) × Kelly Fraction

  This ensures:
   ✓ Never risk more than 4% on one trade
   ✓ Cap at 0.25% for safety
   ✓ Optimal compounding via Kelly criterion
   ✓ Position size scales with probability
